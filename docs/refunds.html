Refund Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Cancellation

Subscriptions are month-to-month. You may request cancellation at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not remove access already delivered for the current billing period unless required by law.

Refunds

If an early-access build is unavailable or cannot be delivered after payment, contact us promptly for a full refund or credit. We will also review requests for a refund made within 30 days of payment when the delivered build is materially unusable for its stated purpose.

Refunds are issued through Paddle.com, the Merchant of Record, to the original payment method where possible. Processing or currency-conversion timing may depend on the payment provider.

How to request a refund

Email cowwoc2020@gmail.com with the purchaser email address, transaction or receipt identifier, and a short description of the issue. We will acknowledge the request and coordinate the resolution with Paddle.

Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights.
